Verification & legalisation

Notary Attestation

Notarial certification guidance for documents that need formal witnessing or certification.

The exact authentication chain depends on the document type, issuing authority, issuing state or country, destination and purpose. Educational certificates, personal records and commercial documents may follow different routes. Notarial certification guidance for documents that need formal witnessing or certification.

When this may be needed

Affidavits, powers of attorney, declarations and selected personal or commercial documents.

Information to prepare

Prepare the original document or the format requested by the receiving authority, issuer or institution details, applicant identity information, destination country and the purpose for which the document will be used.

Before you proceed

Confirm the exact route.

Notary Attestation requirements can vary by issuing authority and receiving organisation. Check the latest official instructions before sending original documents.

  • Confirm the destination and purpose.
  • Identify the authority that issued the document.
  • Check whether prior verification or translation is required.
  • Confirm current fees and timelines with the relevant authority.
