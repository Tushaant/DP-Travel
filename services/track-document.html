Track Your Document

Request an update on a document or application currently being handled by DP Travel Solutions.

The exact authentication chain depends on the document type, issuing authority, issuing state or country, destination and purpose. Educational certificates, personal records and commercial documents may follow different routes. Request an update on a document or application currently being handled by DP Travel Solutions.

When this may be needed

Existing customers seeking an update on a submitted document or application.

Information to prepare

Prepare the original document or the format requested by the receiving authority, issuer or institution details, applicant identity information, destination country and the purpose for which the document will be used.

Before you proceed

Confirm the exact route.

Track Your Document requirements can vary by issuing authority and receiving organisation. Check the latest official instructions before sending original documents.

  • Confirm the destination and purpose.
  • Identify the authority that issued the document.
  • Check whether prior verification or translation is required.
  • Confirm current fees and timelines with the relevant authority.
