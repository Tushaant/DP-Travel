Verification & Document Services

Explore document authentication, apostille, primary-source verification, notarial and credential-assessment services.

This service family covers document verification and legalisation pathways that may be requested by universities, employers, professional regulators or overseas authorities. The exact process depends on the receiving organisation and the document's issuing authority.

When these services may be needed

Credential checks, professional registration, education, employment and official cross-border documentation.

Information to prepare

The receiving organisation's instructions, original document or required copy, issuer details, applicant identity and destination.

Before you proceed

Confirm the exact route.

Requirements vary by document, destination and receiving authority. Confirm whether the request is for verification, attestation, apostille or credential evaluation before submitting originals.

  • Identify the receiving organisation.
  • Check its accepted verification provider.
  • Confirm the required format and delivery route.
  • Check current fees and timelines.
