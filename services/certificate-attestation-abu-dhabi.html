Attestation by destination

Certificate Attestation – Abu Dhabi

Guidance for certificates intended for official use in Abu Dhabi.

The exact authentication chain depends on the document type, issuing authority, issuing state or country, destination and purpose. Educational certificates, personal records and commercial documents may follow different routes. Guidance for certificates intended for official use in Abu Dhabi.

When this may be needed

Employment, higher education and family applications.

Information to prepare

Prepare the original document or the format requested by the receiving authority, issuer or institution details, applicant identity information, destination country and the purpose for which the document will be used.

Before you proceed

Confirm the exact route.

Certificate Attestation – Abu Dhabi requirements can vary by issuing authority and receiving organisation. Check the latest official instructions before sending original documents.

  • Confirm the destination and purpose.
  • Identify the authority that issued the document.
  • Check whether prior verification or translation is required.
  • Confirm current fees and timelines with the relevant authority.
