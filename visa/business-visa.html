Business Visa & Business Visitor Guidance

Travelling overseas for meetings, conferences, negotiations or commercial events? Understand the distinction between business-visitor activities and employment before applying.

Discuss your plans ↗View document checklist ↓

Understanding this visa category

A business visitor category may cover defined short-term activities such as attending meetings, conferences, trade events or negotiating contracts. It does not automatically permit local employment, paid services or productive work. The permitted activities are destination-specific.

Visa labels, eligibility criteria, permitted activities and supporting evidence differ between destinations and can change. Treat this page as a general orientation guide, not a country-specific eligibility assessment.

Who may need this route?

  • Employees attending meetings with overseas clients or partners
  • Business owners exploring commercial opportunities
  • Delegates attending eligible conferences, trade fairs or official events

Documents commonly considered

The list below is an initial preparation guide only. The official application checklist for your destination and circumstances takes precedence.

  • Valid passport and application details
  • Invitation letter from the host organisation, if applicable
  • Employer letter explaining the purpose, dates and funding of travel
  • Conference or event registration
  • Meeting schedule and business itinerary
  • Evidence of current employment or business activity
  • Financial arrangements and accommodation details, where required

A practical application journey

  1. Describe the exact activities planned during the visit
  2. Check whether those activities fit the destination’s business visitor category
  3. Confirm whether a work permit or another visa category is required
  4. Collect host invitation, employer letter and event evidence
  5. Submit the application through the official channel
  6. Review visa conditions before travelling

Common application mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming all business travel is covered by a visitor category
  • Confusing meetings or negotiations with taking up local employment
  • Using a generic invitation without dates, purpose or host details
  • Failing to explain who pays for the trip and the applicant’s role
  • Overlooking restrictions on remuneration or providing services

Frequently asked questions

Can I work on a business visitor visa?

Not necessarily. Business visitor categories commonly restrict local employment and the provision of services. Check the precise conditions for the destination.

Is an invitation letter always required?

Requirements differ. A host invitation or event registration may be useful or specifically requested for some routes.

Can I attend a conference?

Some business visitor categories permit conference attendance, subject to conditions. Confirm the event type and any payment restrictions with the official authority.

What if I will be doing hands-on work overseas?

A different work-authorisation category may be necessary. Do not rely on a business visitor visa without checking the rules.
